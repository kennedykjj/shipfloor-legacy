### Decisions the pipeline took
- `shipfloor-config.sh machine ...` no longer needs a registered project: the machine config belongs to the machine, and the installer asks from anywhere.
- One helper, `shipfloor_config.dispatcher_every`, reads the pace for the dashboard and the health history (a bool or a bad value falls back to 3).
- The page keeps the "Windows only" reason as its own constant too, so a disabled control always shows a reason even if the server sends none.
- The 409 sentence drops one trailing period from the system's reason so it does not end in "..".

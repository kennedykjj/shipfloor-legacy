### For validation to look at
- **One deviation from the plan.** The plan gave `.yours` `min-height: auto`. The engineer did not use it: with a scrolling `#list` inside, `auto` resolves to the whole list and "Yours" would never shrink. Instead `.yours:not(.maxed)` has `min-height: var(--ymin, 0px)` and `fit()` sets `--ymin` to the heading, the first two cards and the key hints when they show. The flex values are the spec's (`.yours` `flex: 0 1000 auto`, `.telem` `flex: 0 1 auto`, floor 100px, `max-height: 60%`).
- **`--ymin` is 1px short.** It counts the panel's top border but not its bottom one. The list's own floor (`--two`) still holds, so the cards are not cut; at most the key hints sit 1px into the panel's bottom padding. Not sent back for a round.
- **Today with little content is smaller than before.** Its 292px minimum is now a 100px floor, so a Today that says only "No run has ended today." takes its content height where it used to take 292px. The spec's "Architecture" asks for this floor; its "UX" sentence on "at least 292px" when there is room is the one to check against.
- **At 1366x768 with both open,** `.tight` needs the heading, two cards, the 10px gap and 100px of Today to fit the column. If they do not, the column overflows rather than cutting a card. This is the first number to measure.
- **`fit()` runs on every scroll event** of the list, Today and the tiles, and takes `.tight` off and on inside the same call to measure. It is one forced layout per event; worth a look on a slow machine.
- **A spacing slip** in `fit()`: `side.clientHeight <chrome` lacks a space. Cosmetic.
